# Token icons for the Aave panel

Local copies, resized to 96x96 PNG. Nothing is fetched from the network at runtime.

- WETH, wstETH, weETH, EURC, USDG, GHO: Trust Wallet assets repo
  (https://github.com/trustwallet/assets, `blockchains/ethereum/assets/<checksum address>/logo.png`), MIT licensed repo.
- cbBTC, RLUSD, frxUSD: SmolDapp tokenAssets
  (https://github.com/SmolDapp/tokenAssets, `tokens/1/<address>/logo-128.png`), MIT licensed repo.

The repositories are MIT licensed. The logos themselves remain trademarks of their issuers
and are used here only to identify the token.
